# 🚀 Initiative: The Sabi Ecosystem Launchpad (`sabiai.work`)

## 1. Product Vision & Seed Brief
**"Sabi"** `/’sah-bee/` *(v.) — To understand deeply.*

The **Sabi Ecosystem Launchpad** (`https://sabiai.work/`) is the central entry point and discovery portal for applications built under the Sabi AI umbrella. Its mission is to showcase purpose-built tools that celebrate local cultural identity on a global scale while empowering niche productivity and community wellness.

## 2. Core Ecosystem Pods
1. **Logic & Productivity:**
   - **Refine** (`https://prompts.sabiai.work/`): Precision prompt engineering and structured AI workflow refinement.
   - **Facilitator** (`https://facilitator.sabiai.work/`): Agile coaching, retrospective facilitation, and team alignment tools.
2. **Culture & Context:**
   - **NaijaSpeak** (`https://naijaspeak.sabiai.work/`): Interactive Nigerian Pidgin translation and cultural context engine.
   - **Mile 2 Radio** (`https://m2r.sabiai.work/dashboard`): Live Afrobeat broadcasting and diaspora community radio.
   - **Our Mumu Don Do** (`https://our-mumu-don-do.sabiai.work/`): Interactive audio-visual Nigerian history, critical thinking, and civic storytelling.
3. **Community & Wellness:**
   - **Aqualog** (`https://aqualog.sabiai.work/`): Coldwater swimming tracker and wellness log.
   - **G-Kicker Coach** (`https://coach.g-kicker.sabiai.work/`): SFV-compliant G-Junioren football training planner powered by generative AI.
   - **Kids Cup Helpers**: Volunteer coordination for youth sports tournaments.

## 3. Core Architectural & UX Goals
- **AI Crawler & SEO Readiness:** Full OpenGraph, Schema.org `WebSite` & `ItemList` JSON-LD structured data, dynamic `sitemap.xml`, and `robots.txt` explicitly welcoming AI search crawlers (`GPTBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `PerplexityBot`, `Google-Extended`).
- **Shift-Left Repository Boundary:** All catalog and data access encapsulated in `src/repositories/` to keep UI components pure and testable.
- **Zero-Friction Navigation:** Sub-second page loads with accessible cards, clear status badges, and direct deep-links to each ecosystem app.
